# Grilling

Vendored and adapted from Matt Pocock's `grilling` skill under the MIT license in `docs/licenses/mattpocock-skills-MIT.txt`.

Interview the supervisor relentlessly until the design reaches shared understanding.
Walk each branch of the decision tree and resolve dependencies one by one.
Give a recommended answer with every question.

Ask exactly one question at a time and wait for its answer before continuing.
Multiple questions at once are bewildering and are forbidden by the Firstmate design profile.

Find facts by exploring the repository and available evidence rather than asking.
Put actual decisions to the supervisor and wait.
Do not act on the resulting design until the supervisor confirms shared understanding.

When the enclosing Firstmate design profile supplies a stricter status, context, or escalation contract, that contract controls how each question is sent and answered.
